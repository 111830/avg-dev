## The Origin and Philosophy
This project began from nothing. It started when I came across a video from a Senior Software Engineer at Netflix. Despite his incredible expertise, the video had very few views—around 200 to 500—likely because most people today lack the patience to sit through a high-level, one-hour technical deep dive. After doing some research to understand who this person was and how he operated, I realized I was watching someone who truly commands the machine. 

Seeing that level of mastery changed my path. It pushed me to stop copying surface-level tutorials and start improving my own technical thinking by following the methods of those who understand computers at their core. This repository is no longer just a collection of commands; it is a documentation of my journey from simple workstation aesthetics to deep technical competence.

## Moving Beyond the Surface
The modern industry is saturated with high-level languages and frameworks like JavaScript, HTML, PHP, C#, React, or .NET. While these tools are useful for web development and standard software, they often stay on the surface. They hide what is actually happening "under the hood" and prevent a developer from truly understanding how a machine thinks and processes logic.

My focus has shifted entirely to the foundations of Software Engineering. To build things like the Linux kernel, web browsers, compilers, or advanced database systems, one must master low-level languages:
* **C and C++:** To understand memory management and performance.
* **Rust:** To explore modern systems safety and concurrency.
* **Operating Systems and Architecture:** To understand the bridge between code and silicon.

This is what defines a real Software Engineer—the ability to build the tools that others use to build apps.

## Future Vision: Avg-Sys and Avg-Math

Hardware, systems, and mathematics are three sides of the same coin. Once the foundations above are solid, this journey branches into two specialized paths:

1. **avg-sys:** A deeper dive into systems programming and architecture — kernels, compilers, and the low-level infrastructure that everything else runs on, moving from understanding the machine to actually building on top of it.
2. **avg-math:** Focused on converting complex mathematical theorems into algorithmic logic. I will use languages like R and Matlab to handle statistical modeling and advanced calculus, ensuring that the mathematics isn't just theoretical but functional.

## Documentation and Efficiency
I document everything I learn from books, including exercises and theorems. Because my focus is on the logic and the "how-to" of the computer, I use AI tools specifically for generating LaTeX boilerplate and complex TikZ graphics. This allows me to focus my time on understanding the core concepts rather than spending hours on document formatting. Efficiency is key when mastering the depths of computer science.
